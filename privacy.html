Privacy policy

Last updated November 19, 2026.

Kitchen display is a private screen used by one household. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar: read only. It reads the household's calendars to show upcoming events.
  • Google Tasks: it reads the household's lists and adds items or new lists when a family member asks. It does not delete anything.
  • Gmail (the household's shared inbox): it reads emails a family member asks for, to print them or to draft a reply. It sends a reply only after a family member approves it. It does not delete, move or change any email.

Where the data goes

The data is shown on a screen in the household's home and stays on that household's own computer, except that when a family member asks the kitchen assistant something, the text needed to answer (including any email asked about) is sent to Anthropic's Claude to prepare the answer. It is not sold and not used for advertising.

Google's policy

Kitchen display's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's linked apps page: choose Kitchen display and remove its access.
